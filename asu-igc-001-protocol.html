ASU-IGC-001 · PHASE 3

Controlled research protocol

A public method for developing, testing, challenging and revising the Intergenerational Goal Continuity and Social Evolution framework.

Status: active research protocol. It does not establish that the theory is correct, does not report a completed systematic review and has not yet been independently peer reviewed. Download the controlled PDF.
PURPOSE AND SCOPE

Turn a developing idea into a testable research programme

The protocol separates terminology, propositions, evidence status, competing explanations, disconfirmation criteria, literature review, ethics, revision and external review. The principal theory wording remains published at asultd.com.

Numbered propositions

IDPropositionCurrent statusWhat could weaken it
P01Recurring family narratives, expectations and practices may influence later priorities through learning and socialisation.Working hypothesisNo association after credible controls, or better explanation by unrelated factors.
P02Social, institutional, economic and historical conditions may moderate whether continuity strengthens, changes or ends.Working hypothesisNo meaningful moderation across well-designed comparative studies.
P03Separation, rejection, mobility, new relationships and institutional change may redirect or interrupt continuity.Working hypothesisPredicted disruptions do not alter measured outcomes.
P04AI-rich environments may change which cognitive skills are practised, delegated, rewarded or neglected.Open empirical questionLongitudinal evidence shows no relevant change or effects are better explained elsewhere.
P05Many interacting personal and group trajectories may contribute to changes in institutions, norms and collective priorities.Broad working hypothesisIndividual-level mechanisms do not scale or aggregate as predicted.

Required comparisons

  • Social learning and cultural evolution
  • Developmental psychology and family systems
  • Socioeconomic persistence and institutional effects
  • Behavioural genetics and gene–environment correlation, without inferring heredity from correlation
  • Selection, reporting, memory and survivorship bias
  • Chance, individual agency and within-family variation

Evidence thresholds

01

Source quality

Prefer systematic reviews, peer-reviewed research, official statistics and primary legal or policy sources appropriate to the claim.

02

Traceability

Record the exact claim, source, date, population, method, limitation and relevance.

03

Disconfirmation

Log contrary and null findings with the same discipline as supportive findings.

04

Revision

Change or retire propositions when the evidence requires it, preserving the prior record.

Research boundaries

Research involving people or sensitive records requires consent, privacy protection, data minimisation, safeguarding and independent ethical review where applicable. The long-term biological extension remains speculative and separate from the near-term cultural and behavioural research programme.
