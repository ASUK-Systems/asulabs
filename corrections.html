Correction policy

Factual errors should be corrected promptly where practicable. Substantive revisions should preserve the existence of the previous version and explain the reason for the change where useful. Unsupported claims should be reduced, qualified or withdrawn rather than defended simply because they were published.

Challenge the record

Send a specific source, legal point, factual correction or authorship objection to asulabs@proton.me. Submissions are assessed on evidence and relevance.
