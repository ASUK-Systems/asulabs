# ASU Labs — Energetic Materials Research Scope

**Status:** Public research-policy document  
**Jurisdictional focus:** Great Britain / UK  
**Review date:** 15 August 2026

## Purpose

ASU Labs may document energetic-material subjects for scientific, historical, theoretical and regulatory research.

This publication policy deliberately excludes operational information that could materially facilitate explosive manufacture.

## Publication boundary

ASU Labs does not publish:

- explosive formulation ratios;
- batch calculations;
- manufacturing recipes;
- step-by-step synthesis or preparation procedures;
- optimisation methods;
- ignition/initiation designs;
- confinement methods;
- procurement/evasion guidance intended to bypass controls.

## UK regulatory note

HSE states that most explosive manufacturing activities require a licence and specifically identifies gunpowder manufacture as manufacturing under the Explosives Regulations 2014.

HSE also states that acquisition and/or keeping of black powder requires an explosives certificate. Storage requirements are separately governed by the Explosives Regulations 2014.

These statements should be rechecked against current official guidance before relying on them.

This is not legal advice and does not create an explosives licence, certificate, exemption or other authorisation. Professional advice should be obtained where practical work has meaningful legal, safety, regulatory or commercial consequences.
