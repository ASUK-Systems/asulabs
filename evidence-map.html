How entries become assessable

  1. Record a narrow claim and proposition ID.
  2. Add a stable citation and source type.
  3. Extract method, population, result and limitation.
  4. State whether the source supports, challenges or is neutral.
  5. Record an independent confidence judgement with reasons.
  6. Update the proposition assessment only after contrary evidence is also reviewed.
