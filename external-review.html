INDEPENDENT SCRUTINY

External review status

A transparent distinction between invitation, feedback, professional review and peer review.

Current status: no independent peer review, academic endorsement or institutional validation is claimed.

Review sought

  • Conceptual and terminology review from relevant academic disciplines
  • Methods review covering testability, confounding, measurement and bias
  • Ethics and data-protection review before any participant research
  • Legal and equality review for applied policy proposals
  • Lived-experience and practitioner challenge where proposals affect people or services

Publication rule

Material feedback should be logged with the reviewer’s permitted attribution, scope, date, response and resulting revision. Private correspondence will not be published without permission. Silence, contact or informal comment will not be represented as endorsement.
