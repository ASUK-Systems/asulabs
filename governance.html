GOVERNANCE

Safety and responsibility are gates.

A warning paragraph is not a risk control. Where an activity is unlawful, inadequately controlled or beyond competence, the correct action is to stop.

01 — Stop

Stop where legal authority, competence, facilities, supervision or adequate controls cannot be established.

02 — Assess

Identify hazards, exposure, people affected, equipment risks and credible failure modes.

03 — Control

Prefer elimination, substitution, containment, engineering and procedural controls before relying on PPE.

04 — Record

Record decisions, sources, incidents, near misses and review dates. Reassess when circumstances change.

Higher-risk subjects

ASU Labs does not publish operational instructions that could materially increase the ability to cause harm. For chemical, pressure, electrical, biological or machinery-related work, the appropriate route may be formal training, qualified supervision, an appropriate facility, professional advice or not proceeding. See the dedicated Research Safety & Hazardous Materials framework for the higher-risk gate.

HSE's risk-assessment framework uses hazard identification, risk assessment, risk control, recording and review as core steps.

Research ethics

Human research should use appropriate consent, privacy safeguards and independent ethical review where required. Policy work should distinguish groups and legal categories accurately and should not use stereotypes as evidence.
