STRUCTURED REVIEW

Literature review method

A reproducible route from research question to evidence record, including contrary and null findings.

Status: protocol published; searches and screening not yet represented as complete.

Review sequence

01

Define

Fix the proposition, population, exposure or mechanism, comparator and outcome before searching.

02

Search

Record databases, search strings, dates, filters and supplementary source routes.

03

Screen

Apply written inclusion and exclusion criteria and retain exclusion reasons.

04

Extract

Capture design, sample, measures, findings, limitations, funding and relevance.

05

Appraise

Assess source quality and fit to the exact claim without converting association into causation.

06

Synthesise

Report convergence, inconsistency, uncertainty, gaps and credible alternative explanations.

Initial inclusion principles

  • Direct relevance to a numbered proposition or competing explanation.
  • Methods and population described well enough to assess applicability.
  • Original research, systematic review, scholarly theory paper or authoritative official evidence.
  • Contrary, null and replication evidence retained rather than filtered out.
  • Publication date is not used as a proxy for quality, although currency is considered.

Minimum extraction fields

Source ID; proposition ID; full citation; stable link or DOI; source type; research question; jurisdiction; population; design; sample; measures; main result; effect or uncertainty where reported; limitations; conflicts or funding; support/challenge/neutral classification; reviewer note; review date.
