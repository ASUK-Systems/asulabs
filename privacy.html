PRIVACY

Privacy and data-minimisation framework

Public-site principle

The public site should collect as little personal information as possible. If a contact form, analytics, newsletter or other processing is added, the privacy notice must be updated to describe the real arrangement.

Keep private records private

Do not publish home addresses, identity documents, private correspondence, credentials, financial information or sensitive personal information merely to make the project appear more official.

Implementation: Complete this page against the actual hosting, analytics, cookies and contact systems before collecting personal data. ICO privacy-notice guidance
