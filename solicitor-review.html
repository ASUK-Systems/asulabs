PROFESSIONAL REVIEW PACK

Give a solicitor the questions, records and gaps.

Questions to answer

  1. Which works are owned personally and which are owned, licensed or assigned to an entity?
  2. Are the ASU names, logos and domains worth trade-mark protection?
  3. Does any unpublished technical work justify patent or registered-design advice?
  4. Which materials should be treated as confidential and protected by NDA?
  5. Are the website terms sufficiently precise for the intended use?
  6. Are there employment, contractor, commissioned-work or collaboration issues affecting ownership?
  7. Which assignments or licences are required?
  8. What evidence should be preserved in case of challenge or dispute?

Evidence to provide

  • Original source files and earliest drafts.
  • Git repositories and commit history.
  • Domain registrations and hosting records.
  • Dated website versions and revision logs.
  • Design/source files and software repositories.
  • Contracts, assignments, licences and NDAs.
  • Third-party material and licence records.
  • Contribution/ownership schedule for collaborative work.

Desired professional output

A written ownership map, recommended registrations, confidentiality strategy, contract/assignment requirements, website-terms review and a concise list of evidential gaps to close.
